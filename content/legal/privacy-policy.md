## 12. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, taking into account our operational requirements and any applicable legal, accounting, taxation, regulatory, contractual, dispute-resolution, or record-retention obligations.

Our current operational retention periods include:

### Enquiries and leads

Enquiry and lead information is generally retained for up to **90 days**.

### Newsletter information

Newsletter subscription information is generally retained for up to **365 days**, unless you unsubscribe earlier.

Limited information may be retained longer where necessary to demonstrate consent or maintain a suppression record so that an unsubscribe request continues to be respected.

### Client and project working records

Operational client and project working records are generally scheduled for deletion within **7 days** after the relevant project activity is completed where the information is no longer required.

This 7-day period does not apply where particular records must be retained for longer, including contracts, transaction records, invoices, acceptance evidence, legal records, consent evidence, records relevant to a dispute, or records required by law.

### Payment and transaction information

Routine operational payment information may generally be retained for up to **90 days** where no longer required for the relevant transaction.

Accounting, taxation, corporate, invoice, payment, and supporting records are retained for longer where required by applicable law.

### Legal and dispute records

We may retain relevant information for longer where reasonably necessary to:

- comply with legal obligations;
- establish or defend legal rights;
- resolve disputes;
- investigate fraud or abuse; or
- preserve required business records.

When information is no longer required, we may delete, anonymise, or otherwise dispose of it as appropriate.
