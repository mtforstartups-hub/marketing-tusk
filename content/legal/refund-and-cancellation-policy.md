If you cancel an eligible service **before Marketing Tusk has begun performing the agreed work**, you may request a refund of the amount paid for the affected service.

**connect@marketingtusk.com**

## 3. Cancellation After Work Begins

Once Marketing Tusk has begun performing the agreed services, amounts paid are **generally non-refundable**.

This reflects the allocation of professional time, strategy, research, creative work, planning, resources, and other work undertaken in connection with the engagement.

Where appropriate, Marketing Tusk may consider a **partial refund on a case-by-case basis**.

Any discretionary refund will depend on the circumstances of the engagement, including the work already performed and the applicable service terms.

Nothing in this section excludes a refund or other remedy that Marketing Tusk is required to provide under applicable law.

## 4. Subscriptions and Recurring Services

Marketing Tusk may offer recurring services or retainers billed:

**monthly, quarterly, or annually.**

Where a service is identified as automatically renewing, it will renew for the applicable billing period until cancelled.

You may request cancellation by emailing:

**connect@marketingtusk.com**

Unless different terms have been expressly agreed for the relevant service, cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current billing period**.

Cancellation prevents the next applicable renewal. It does not automatically create a right to a refund for services already provided or for amounts that are otherwise non-refundable under this Policy.

Any refund request connected with an active subscription will be assessed under the other provisions of this Policy and any applicable mandatory rights.

## 5. Seven-Day Free Trial

Where Marketing Tusk offers an eligible **7-day free trial**, the trial does **not automatically convert into a paid subscription**.

At the end of the trial, you must take the required affirmative action to purchase or activate the applicable paid service.

Because no automatic paid conversion occurs, merely allowing a free trial to expire does not create a paid subscription charge.

Marketing Tusk may approve a partial refund on a case-by-case basis after considering the circumstances of the engagement.

A prior discretionary refund does not create an entitlement to the same treatment for another transaction or customer.

Any statutory right to a refund or other remedy remains unaffected.
