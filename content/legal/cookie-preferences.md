# Cookie Preferences

You can choose which optional technologies Marketing Tusk may use. Necessary technologies cannot be switched off where they are genuinely required to operate, secure or provide requested functionality on the website.

You can change these preferences at any time.

### Necessary

**Always Active**

These technologies are required for essential website operation, security or functionality requested by you.

They are not used for optional advertising or analytics purposes.

### Analytics

**Default: Off**

Analytics technologies help us understand how visitors use marketingtusk.com, including website traffic, page interactions and site performance.

Current technologies in this category include:

Google Analytics / GA4  
Microsoft Clarity

These technologies will not be activated where prior consent is required unless you enable Analytics.

### Advertising & Targeting

**Default: Off**

These technologies help us measure advertising campaigns, understand conversions and support advertising or remarketing activities.

Current technologies in this category include:

Google Ads  
Meta Pixel  
LinkedIn Insight Tag

These technologies will not be activated where prior consent is required unless you enable Advertising & Targeting.

### Controls

Save Preferences

Reject Non-Essential

Accept All

You can return to Cookie Settings at any time to change or withdraw your choices.

For more information, see our Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy.
