**Effective Date: 10 September 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how **PRANTUSHSHRA MARKETING PRIVATE LIMITED**, operating under the brand **Marketing Tusk** ("Marketing Tusk", "we", "us", or "our"), uses cookies, pixels, tags and similar technologies on **marketingtusk.com**.

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

## 1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored on or accessed from your browser or device when you visit a website.

We may also use similar technologies, including:

- pixels;
- tags;
- local-storage technologies;
- scripts; and
- other technologies used for analytics, advertising, security, measurement or website functionality.

For simplicity, we refer to these collectively as "cookies and similar technologies" in this Policy.

## 2. Why We Use These Technologies

Depending on your location and choices, we may use cookies and similar technologies to:

- operate and maintain our website;
- provide functionality requested by users;
- protect the website and detect misuse;
- understand website usage and performance;
- measure traffic and engagement;
- understand how visitors arrive at our website;
- measure advertising campaigns;
- attribute enquiries or conversions to marketing campaigns;
- support advertising and remarketing activities; and
- improve our website and marketing effectiveness.

## 3. Categories of Technologies We Use

### Strictly Necessary

These technologies are used where necessary for the operation, security or functionality of the website or to provide functionality specifically requested by you.

Where a technology is genuinely strictly necessary, it may operate without optional consent where permitted by applicable law.

### Analytics and Measurement

Analytics technologies help us understand how visitors interact with marketingtusk.com.

They may process information such as:

- pages viewed;
- session activity;
- browser and device information;
- approximate location derived from technical information;
- referral information;
- IP address;
- interaction events; and
- other usage information.

Where applicable law requires prior consent, analytics technologies remain disabled until you provide the relevant consent.

### Advertising and Targeting

Advertising technologies may help us:

- measure advertising campaigns;
- understand whether advertisements lead to website interactions;
- create or measure advertising audiences;
- perform campaign attribution; and
- support advertising or remarketing activities.

These technologies may use identifiers or interaction information that allows website activity to be associated with advertising systems.

Where prior consent is required, advertising and targeting technologies remain disabled until you provide that consent.

## 6. Your Cookie Choices

Where applicable law requires your consent before non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used, we provide a consent mechanism that allows you to make choices before those technologies are activated.

Depending on the applicable configuration, you may be offered options such as:

**Accept All**

Allows permitted non-essential analytics and advertising technologies to operate.

**Reject Non-Essential**

Keeps technologies that require optional consent disabled.

**Manage Preferences**

Allows you to control individual categories of non-essential technologies.

Not providing optional consent should not prevent you from accessing website functionality that does not depend on those technologies.

## 7. Changing or Withdrawing Your Preferences

Where consent controls apply, you may change or withdraw your cookie preferences after making an initial choice.

Changing your preference affects the future operation of technologies controlled through our consent system.

Withdrawal of consent does not make processing that occurred lawfully before withdrawal unlawful.
